RESEARCH

Methodology

Market-aware projections

V12 compares historical player production with the sportsbook market line and identifies discrepancies between its projection and the posted line.

Uncertainty matters

Historical player volatility is used as an uncertainty filter. A projection must satisfy the frozen research criteria before appearing as qualified.

Frozen before results

Qualifying projections are recorded before results are known, creating a cleaner forward-testing process.

No guaranteed outcomes

Qualification is not a guaranteed result or validated win probability. Forward results are used to evaluate the system.

IMPORTANT

Qualification identifies lower-uncertainty model situations with a meaningful model-to-market discrepancy. It does not, by itself, establish a validated directional edge or guarantee an outcome.

BEFORE RESULTS

Freeze first.

Market lines, model projections, qualification status, and supporting model values are recorded before results are known.

AFTER RESULTS

Grade without rewriting history.

Actual results are added afterward. Frozen projections remain unchanged so historical performance can be evaluated against what was known beforehand.

TRANSPARENCY

Performance Exclusions

Beginning with 2026 NFL Week 3, exclusion handling follows a prospective policy established before results.

DID_NOT_PLAY

No game participation

If a player records no participation, the frozen observation remains in the permanent record but is excluded from normal model-performance calculations.

INJURY_EXIT

Materially shortened opportunity

A documented injury may qualify only when it causes participation to end materially earlier than the player's normal game opportunity. The reason remains documented in the record.

PERFORMANCE ELIGIBLE

Normal game variance stays.

Poor performance, game script, blowouts, coaching decisions, reduced opportunities, weather, and other normal football variance are not grounds for exclusion.

Exclusion does not mean deletion.

Every frozen observation remains visible in the permanent track record, including its market line, model projection, official result when available, and documented exclusion reason.
