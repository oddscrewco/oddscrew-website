ABOUT

Building OddsCrew

Data. Research. Edges.

OddsCrew is an independent sports analytics project focused on transparent, data-driven tools for analyzing sports markets. The current research program begins with NFL player projections and ongoing forward testing.

THE MISSION

Sports analytics built around evidence.

OddsCrew is being built as an independent sports analytics platform focused on transparent model projections, market comparison, and measurable forward performance.

THE IDEA

Build the model. Publish the projection. Freeze the record. Grade the result. Learn from the evidence.

01

DATA

Start with historical performance, current market information, and measurable player-level data rather than narratives alone.

02

RESEARCH

Test ideas against historical and forward results. Features earn their place through evidence rather than assumption.

03

TRANSPARENCY

Freeze projections before outcomes and preserve the results afterward, including observations that do not make the model look better.

WHY ODDSCREW

The market is the benchmark.

A projection is only meaningful when it can be evaluated against something. OddsCrew uses the sports market as a benchmark rather than pretending it does not exist.

MODEL VS. MARKET

Don't just ask whether the projection was right.

Ask whether the model was more accurate than the market benchmark. That comparison is central to how OddsCrew evaluates its research.

BUILT IN PUBLIC

The record stays.

OddsCrew's forward-testing process is designed so that published observations remain inspectable after the games end.

01

Freeze

Projections and qualification status are recorded before results.

02

Grade

Official results are compared with both the model projection and the market line.

03

Preserve

Historical observations remain in the permanent record instead of disappearing after a poor result.

04

Evaluate

Performance is judged across the growing sample rather than isolated outcomes.

CURRENT PRINCIPLE Transparency before certainty.

OddsCrew does not present qualified projections as guaranteed outcomes. The purpose of forward testing is to determine what the evidence actually supports over time.
